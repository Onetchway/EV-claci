Our journey

Twelve years, one site at a time.

We did not arrive at EV infrastructure — we built our way to it, from foundations and floorplates to the fastest-growing charging networks in the country.

2013

Founded on civil construction

NAKJM begins as a construction firm — buildings, floorplates and groundwork — and installs its first EV charger as electric mobility takes its first steps in India.

The first charger
2017

Electriva onboarded

Our first CPO partnership. Multi-station rollouts across Delhi NCR turn one-off installs into a repeatable delivery system.

First CPO partner
2020

Norbeorker joins the roster

Through the toughest year for site work, the network keeps growing — new partner, new programmes, crews fully licensed and night-shift capable.

2021

Terra Charge partnership

Japan-backed Terra Charge brings hub-scale programmes — five charging hubs delivered end to end, civil to commissioning.

Hub-scale delivery
2022

XPulse comes aboard

Six hubs including the Samalkha heavy-duty fleet corridor — 70+ DC fast chargers serving trucks on continuous duty cycles.

2023

300 sites delivered

The milestone that proved the model: three hundred sites executed by the same unified in-house civil and electrical teams.

300-site milestone
2024

ChargZe and Livanto Green

Two more CPO networks choose NAKJM as their execution partner, alongside a Hyderabad-based charger OEM tie-up covering supply, installation, testing and commissioning.

2025

OEM entry — VinFast

Direct OEM programmes begin: VinFast delivery hubs and dealer infrastructure, alongside Tesla Supercharger site works in Gurgaon.

Direct OEM programmes
Today

1,000+ chargers installed

More than a thousand chargers across 300+ sites — residential AC points to 250 kW hubs — delivered by a 50+ strong in-house team. And the next thousand already in the pipeline.

1,000+ chargers
