Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: August 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, use our charging services, or contact us for support.

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Payment information (credit card details, billing address)
  • Vehicle information (make, model, charging preferences)
  • Usage data (charging sessions, locations, duration)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services:

  • Process charging transactions and payments
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Send service updates and promotional communications
  • Improve our charging network and user experience
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except as described in this policy. We may share information with trusted partners who assist us in operating our services, conducting business, or serving our users.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and assessments
  • Access controls and authentication measures
  • Employee training on data protection

5. Your Rights

You have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access and review your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Delete your account and personal information
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Data portability and export

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, analyze usage patterns, and provide personalized content.
