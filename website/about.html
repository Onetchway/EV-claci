About us

An energy-tech company, built to be operated — not just installed

Livanto is an emerging energy-tech company revolutionising the EV charging landscape with comprehensive, in-house-built solutions.

Our mission

We deliver reliable, high-speed EV charging: up to 360 kW today, built to grow tomorrow. Open to every EV, across every state we reach. A trusted partner for daily commutes and long highway runs alike — hardware, software and franchise support built around how India actually drives.

There should be no gaps when it comes to electric travel in India. That's why we're building, operating and constantly growing a network of AC and DC charging stations — for homes and RWAs, for hotels and offices, along highways and inside cities. Every station reports back to one CMS, so uptime and service quality stay consistent everywhere the network reaches.

Our symbol for growth

The leaf at the centre of our mark is deliberate — Livanto Green is, at its core, a bet that clean energy and everyday infrastructure don't have to be in tension. Every station we build is a small act of that same growth: one more site, one less tailpipe, one more household or fleet that can go electric with confidence.

What we do

We build and operate high-speed EV chargers — up to 360 kW — for rapid, reliable charging, with Pan-India reach and scalable infrastructure wherever it's needed.

Build & operate

Hardware designed in-house to withstand Indian heat, humidity and dust — from a 7.4 kW home unit to a 240 kW dual-gun DC fast charger, all OCPP-based for seamless CSMS integration.

Franchise & partner

Two clear ways in: invest and operate a franchise station, or host one on your land and get paid for it — through the CoCo or PoCo model, whichever fits.

Software

A powerful EV software suite for OEMs and fleet operators to monitor and manage charging infrastructure — the same CMS every Livanto station runs on, OCPI and OCPP 1.6J enabled.
