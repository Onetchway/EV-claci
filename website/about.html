Our Journey

From renewable energy to EV infrastructure

  • 2016 – 2018

    Foundation

    Began as a rooftop solar pioneer (Zivah) across four states, strategically pivoting to EV charger development.

  • 2020 – 2022

    Launch & Manufacturing

    Launched our first station in Kerala, incorporated ElectriVa, and opened a 10,000 sq. ft. manufacturing facility.

  • 2023 – 2025

    Scale & International

    Expanded across major northern and central Indian hubs and initiated a global footprint with 60+ points in Nepal.
