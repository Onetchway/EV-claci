Invest and operate a Livanto Green franchise, or host a station on land or premises you already own — we handle the technology and operations either way.

Franchise · PoCo

Franchise Partner

You invest, Livanto Green operates

Own a Livanto Green-branded charging station — you provide the land and capital, we handle installation, software, monitoring and 24×7 operations.

20–35% gross revenue share for standard models, plus a Livanto assured minimum for the first 24 months

Site / Location Partner

You host, we install & operate

Have land, a parking lot, a society or a commercial premises with spare space? Host a Livanto Green station with no operating burden on you.

Revenue share — earn a per-unit cut of every kWh sold, ideal for high-traffic sites, or
Fixed rental — a stable, lease-like income regardless of usage
