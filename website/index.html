Franchise Partner

You invest, Livanto Green operates

Own a Livanto Green-branded charging station — you provide the land and capital, we handle installation, software, monitoring and 24×7 operations.

20–35% gross revenue share for standard models, plus a Livanto assured minimum for the first 24 months

You host, we install & operate
