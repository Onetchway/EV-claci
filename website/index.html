Our journey

A decade of evolution — from solar roots to EV leadership

  1. 2016–2018

    Foundation

    Began as a rooftop-solar pioneer (Zivah) across 4 states, then strategically pivoted into EV charger development.

  2. 2020–2022

    Launch & manufacturing

    Launched our first station in Kerala, incorporated Electriva and opened a 10,000 sq. ft. manufacturing facility.

  3. 2023–2025

    Scale & international

    Expanded across major Northern & Central Indian hubs and went global with 60+ points in Nepal.
